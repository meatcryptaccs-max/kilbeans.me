About

Social, rebuilt for people who think in systems.

Kilbeans started with a simple frustration: every mainstream platform optimizes for the algorithm, not the human. We built the opposite — a campus ecosystem where depth, privacy, and authenticity aren't features, they're the foundation.

We're an independent team of engineers, designers, and campus builders who believe social software should feel sovereign — yours to own, yours to control, yours to leave if you want.

That means no predictive feeds. No ad auctions on your attention. No silent data harvesting. Just a clean, encrypted space for the people you actually want to hear from.

Built for students, researchers, and creators who want to build reputation through substance — not through engagement bait.

Architecture

Built on principles you can verify, not promises you must trust.

01

Honeypot Security

Proprietary logic traps automated scrapers and bots before they ever reach your feed. Authentic interactions, guaranteed at the protocol level.

02

Local-First Data

SQLite on-device means total ownership of your personal history — with lightning-fast campus networking when you're connected.

03

Zero-Drift Feeds

No predictive modeling. A pure, chronological flow of campus updates from the people you actually choose to follow.

04

End-to-End Encryption

Sector messages and DMs are encrypted client-side. We can't read them — and neither can anyone else.

05

Instant Campus Mesh

Optimized peer networking delivers sub-second delivery across the campus — even on congested networks.

06

Privacy by Design

No phone number required. No shadow profile. Anonymous handles supported natively across the platform.
