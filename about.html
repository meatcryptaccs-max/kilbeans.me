About

Social, rebuilt for people who think in systems.

Kilbeans started with a simple frustration: every mainstream platform optimizes for the algorithm, not the human. So we built the opposite — a campus ecosystem where depth, privacy, and authenticity aren't features bolted on later, they're the foundation the whole thing is built on.

Our Aim

To give students, researchers, and creators a social platform that respects their attention, protects their privacy, and rewards substance over performance. No algorithmic manipulation. No data harvesting. No engagement traps. Just a clean, encrypted space where reputation is earned through what you actually contribute — not through how well you play a game you never agreed to.

What Makes Us Different

End-to-end encryption by default. Local-first data storage on your device. Zero ad trackers. Open protocols. And a Honeypot Security system that traps automated scrapers before they ever reach your feed. Every architectural decision we've made starts from the same question: what would this look like if we built it for the user, not the advertiser?

Our Story

2023
Kilbeans begins
Development starts on the first version of the platform. Kilbeans LTD is formed, with a clear thesis: campus social should be sovereign, encrypted, and depth-first.
2024
Core architecture ships
The Honeypot Security system, local-first SQLite storage, and end-to-end encryption layer are all built into the foundation.
2025
Private beta opens
First Android build reaches private testers on campus. Sectors, encrypted messaging, and zero-drift feeds go live.
2026
1,000+ users
Kilbeans crosses 1,000 active users across campuses. Public launch of v2.0 in private beta.

Who We Build For

Students, researchers, and creators who want to build reputation through substance — not through engagement bait. People who want a social feed that respects their time, an inbox that can't be read by anyone else, and a platform that treats them like adults.
